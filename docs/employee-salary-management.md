# Employee Salary Management

Implemented in the existing Laravel 12 / Blade application. The module uses the existing `employees`, `attendances`, `permission_requests`, `leave_requests`, users, role middleware, styling, email notifications and review screens.

## Database and workflow

- `salary_configurations`: employee FK, effective month, basic salary, Sunday incentive, calendar/fixed divisor, fixed divisor value, working minutes, permission allowance, late-arrival block, reporting time, CL allowance and monthly/calendar-year period, Active/Inactive status. Unique employee/effective month; the latest configuration at or before the selected month applies.
- `salary_statements`: unique employee/month, versioned JSON calculation snapshot, other deductions and reason, Draft/Finalized state, finalizing user/time, revision, Pending/Paid state, payment date/reference. Generating a draft replaces its snapshot, never adds deductions to the previous total. Generating a finalized statement returns the existing snapshot unchanged.
- `salary_audits`: append-only application history with employee, acting user, action, reason, before/after JSON and timestamp. Finalization, revision, configuration, adjustment and payment changes are audited. A revision preserves the previous complete statement in the audit before-image.
- Employee foreign keys restrict deletion to retain payroll history. Employee creation/editing remains unchanged. Employees with salary history should be made Inactive rather than deleted, as with the existing lead-history restriction.
- All payroll routes, downloads and mutations are behind `auth` and the existing `admin` middleware (Admin/Owner only). Employee request submission has separate employee-only endpoints and always resolves the employee from the signed-in email; submitted employee IDs, request types and approval statuses cannot override that identity.
- Existing signed MD email approvals are a conflict with authenticated payroll review. For employees with a salary configuration, signed review URLs additionally require a signed-in Admin/Owner. The legacy email review behavior remains for other employees.
- Changes affecting one employee serialize on that employee row inside transactions. Attendance and request saves/deletes use the same lock; request date/time overlaps and duplicate attendance are checked inside it. Existing attendance and payroll DB uniqueness constraints provide a second defense. Profile saves and statement generation/finalization use the same employee lock. No duplicate deduction ledger is generated.

## Calculation rules

1. Calculate in date order for the selected month, from the employee joining date when later than the first. The calculation service is `app/Services/Salary/SalaryCalculator.php`; no controller or view duplicates payroll arithmetic.
2. Daily salary = monthly basic / calendar days or the configured positive fixed divisor. Per-minute salary = daily salary / configured working minutes. Currency totals use integer paise, with deductions rounded at category totals. Individual rates retain six decimal places for display.
3. A weekday check-in strictly after reporting time (default 10:00) consumes one configured block (default 30 minutes) regardless of actual lateness. The allowance defaults to 120 minutes and resets by calculating each month independently. After all blocks have been used, each subsequent unapproved late arrival deducts one daily salary. Configuration requires a whole number of blocks; zero allowance means the first unapproved late arrival deducts a day.
4. An Approved emergency permission covering the interval from reporting time through check-in exempts that late arrival. An unrelated afternoon permission cannot excuse a morning late arrival. Pending/rejected requests do not exempt it. Actual requested minutes are available on the permission model and in statement history; they never consume the late allowance.
5. Sundays never consume permission or incur late penalties. Full Sunday incentive is credited only for full-day attendance on every Sunday of the calendar month. A missed Sunday, Holiday, Week Off, CL or Half Day on Sunday loses the incentive. A mid-month joiner is ineligible for the full-month Sunday incentive. Months with four or five Sundays are calculated from the calendar.
6. Week Off and Absent each deduct one daily salary. Holidays are paid. CL dates are the union of approved Casual Leave requests and admin-recorded Casual Leave attendance, so the same date is never counted twice. CL beyond the allowance deducts one daily salary per day. Annual allowance means calendar year; earlier unique CL dates in that year consume the balance first. Changing the annual allowance applies the effective configuration for that salary month to the year-to-date usage.
7. Legacy `Leave` attendance without approved leave counts as unapproved absence. Existing approved Sick Leave, Earned Leave and Other leave remain paid; approved Unpaid Leave deducts a day. Existing Half Day deducts half a daily salary; a full-day late penalty replaces its half-day penalty rather than stacking with it.
8. A date has at most one attendance/leave deduction. Approved leave takes precedence, while contradictory attendance/approved-leave dates (including Week Off versus CL) are flagged and block finalization. Present/Late/Permission/Half Day records without a check-in also block finalization. `Permission` attendance requires a matching approved request covering reporting time through check-in.
9. Missing records are explicitly listed as Missing, with no assumed deduction. They block finalization. A preview includes the whole month, including future dates, and must not be treated as final payroll. Pending requests also block finalization. Only completed months can be finalized; month boundaries use Asia/Kolkata.
10. Mid-month joining prorates basic salary by eligible calendar days / calendar days in the month. Full-month basic stays unchanged even with a custom daily divisor. Final payable = earned basic + eligible Sunday incentive - itemized deductions - other deductions; payable is floored at zero. Deductions are reported in full even if they exceed gross earnings.

## Operations

Open **Employee Salary Management**, choose a month, configure each employee (with a change reason), manage attendance/Week Off and review requests, then Generate/refresh a statement. Resolve warnings, review deductions, optionally enter other deductions with a reason, and Finalize after month end. Only finalized statements accept payment tracking. Mark Paid with payment date, reference and reason. To correct a finalized statement, authorize a revision with a reason, refresh/review and finalize again. Paid statements require an audited reversal to Pending before revision.

Finalized snapshots are independent of later edits to employee details, salary rules, attendance or requests. Historical revisions and payment changes remain in the audit history. Draft snapshots reflect the most recent explicit generation/refresh; the dashboard calculates live previews for drafts. Exports use the displayed saved statement snapshot.

Statement details include employee information, rates, attendance and Sunday counts, permission and CL usage/balances, daily deduction reasons, individual deductions, requests, history and payment information. XLSX downloads use existing PhpSpreadsheet and explicit string cells to prevent formula injection. PDF downloads use Dompdf with local fonts and remote resources disabled. Both downloads are authorized and sent with private/no-store caching.

## Deployment and validation

Dependencies: `composer install` (adds Dompdf 3.1 alongside existing PhpSpreadsheet). Apply the additive migration with `php artisan migrate`. No salaries are prefilled, and no employee or attendance records are migrated or duplicated. Configure salary rules before payroll calculation. A rollback drops payroll tables and their history, so it is unsuitable after live payroll use without a retained backup.

Run `php artisan test` for the full suite, or `php artisan test --filter=SalaryManagementTest` for payroll calculation, request, authorization, statement lifecycle and export scenarios. Tests use an isolated in-memory SQLite database. They cover normal attendance, all/missing Sundays, fixed/calendar divisors, leap year, joining proration, six late arrivals, month/year resets, approved/rejected/unrelated permission, monthly/annual excess CL, Week Off, absence, legacy half days/paid/unpaid leave, missing/conflicting/pending attendance, immutable finalization, audited revisions/adjustments/payments, duplicate regeneration, overlapping requests, ownership enforcement, restricted routes, PDF and XLSX.
